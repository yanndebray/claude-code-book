## Tagline

"How I Met Claude Code" invites product managers, developers, and curious minds alike to explore what happens when AI becomes a thinking partner rather than just a tool. This is an honest account of a collaboration that changed how one person works, builds, and thinks about the future.

## Description

In the twilight of the ChatGPT era, a product manager was given access to a pilot program for Claude Code. What was meant to be a three-month evaluation turned into a book — and a transformation in how he understood the relationship between humans and artificial intelligence.

From strategic memos sharpened through intellectual sparring, to CLI tools named after philosophers like Nietzsche and Montaigne, to competitive intelligence that collapsed weeks of research into hours — this memoir traces the arc from curious skeptic to committed collaborator. Along the way, it confronts the real tensions: the colleague whose six months of work was replicated in thirty minutes, the governance gray areas no one has answers to, and the uncomfortable question of what remains distinctly human when intelligence becomes cheap.

This book is an odyssey through these profound questions. It identifies five modes of human-AI collaboration and argues that the shift from AI-as-tool to AI-as-thinking-partner is not just a productivity upgrade. It is an identity shift.

## Quote

"There's BC — Before ChatGPT. There's BCC — Before Claude Code. And now there's whatever comes next."
